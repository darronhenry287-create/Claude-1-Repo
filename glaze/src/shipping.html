Shipping & Returns

Getting your glow to you.

Shipping

Free standard shipping on orders over $35 and on every trio (3-pack). Orders below $35 ship for a flat $4.95.

Orders are processed and dispatched within 1–2 business days. Standard delivery typically takes 3–7 business days depending on your location. You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

Returns

Not glowing? You can return your balm within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, even if it's been opened and tried. Items must be in otherwise resalable condition.

To start a return, email hello@glaze.shop with your order number and we'll send a prepaid label.

Damaged or wrong item

If anything arrives damaged or incorrect, contact us within 7 days and we'll make it right — no need to send it back.
