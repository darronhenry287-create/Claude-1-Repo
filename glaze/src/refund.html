Refund Policy

Love it or return it.

We want you glazed and happy. If you're not, here's how refunds work.

30-day returns

Return your order within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the product price. Opened and tried is fine — we'd rather you actually test it.

How to request a refund

  • Email hello@glaze.shop with your order number.
  • We'll send a prepaid return label.
  • Once your return is received, we'll refund your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

Non-refundable

Original shipping charges (if any) are non-refundable. Gift cards are non-refundable.
