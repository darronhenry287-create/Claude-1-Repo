Refund Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · This is a starting template — please review with legal counsel before launch.

We want you to love your sleep. If you don't, we offer a straightforward refund process.

100-night trial refunds

Cloud Pillows may be returned for a full refund within 100 nights of delivery. Email care@somna.example to begin. Once we receive the item (or confirm donation pickup), we'll refund your original payment method.

Accessory returns

Unused accessories in their original packaging may be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Used or washed items are not eligible unless defective.

Processing time

Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days of approval. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take additional time for the credit to appear on your statement.

Non-refundable items

  • Gift cards
  • Final-sale or clearance items (clearly marked at purchase)
  • Shipping charges on non-defective international returns

Late or missing refunds

If you haven't received a refund after 10 business days, first check with your bank, then contact us and we'll trace it for you.
