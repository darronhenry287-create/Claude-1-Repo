The 5-Year Warranty

We build our products to last — and we stand behind them for years.

What's covered

  • Loss of foam loft or shape beyond normal wear (more than 1" of permanent flattening).
  • Manufacturing defects in the foam core, gel layer, or stitching.
  • Zippers or cover seams that fail under normal use.

What's not covered

  • Normal softening, cosmetic wear, stains, or damage from misuse.
  • Products without proof of purchase from somna.example or an authorized retailer.
  • Comfort preference — that's what the 100-night trial is for.

How to claim

Email care@somna.example with your order number and a photo of the issue. If it's covered, we'll repair or replace the product free of charge. The warranty runs 5 years from the date of delivery and applies to the original purchaser.
