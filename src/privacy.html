Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · This is a starting template, not legal advice. Have it reviewed by a qualified professional and tailor it to your jurisdiction (GDPR, CCPA, etc.) before launch.

SOMNA Sleep Co. ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Order information: name, shipping/billing address, email, phone, and payment details (processed securely by our payment provider — we never store full card numbers).
  • Account information: if you create an account, your login and order history.
  • Usage data: pages visited, device and browser type, and cookies that help the site function and improve.

How we use it

  • To process and ship your orders and provide support.
  • To send order updates and, if you opt in, marketing emails (unsubscribe any time).
  • To improve our products, site, and service, and to prevent fraud.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers who help us operate — payment processors, shipping carriers, email and analytics tools — under agreements that protect your information. We do not sell your personal data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and to opt out of marketing or certain data uses. To exercise these rights, email privacy@somna.example.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to run the store and optional cookies for analytics and marketing. You can control cookies through your browser settings or our cookie banner.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email privacy@somna.example.
