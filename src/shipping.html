Shipping & Returns

Shipping

We offer free 2-day shipping on all orders over $75 within the continental United States. Orders under $75 ship at a flat rate of $6.95. Most orders are processed and dispatched within 24 hours on business days.

  • Standard (US): 2–5 business days
  • Expedited (US): 1–2 business days (rate shown at checkout)
  • International: 5–14 business days to 40+ countries; duties and taxes may apply on delivery

You'll receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships. If your tracking hasn't updated after 3 business days, contact us.

Returns & exchanges

Every pillow comes with our 100-night trial. If you're not satisfied, email care@somna.example within 100 nights of delivery and we'll arrange a free return and full refund. Accessories (sheets, masks) can be returned unused within 30 days.

To start a return, include your order number. We'll send a prepaid label or arrange a pickup. Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days of us receiving the item.

Damaged or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, email us within 14 days with a photo and we'll make it right immediately — no return required for damaged goods.
