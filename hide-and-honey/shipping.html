Last updated: May 2026 · A starting template — review before launch.

Shipping

We offer free shipping on orders over $35 within the continental US. Orders under $35 ship at a flat $4.95. Most orders are processed within 1–2 business days.

  • Standard (US): 3–5 business days
  • Expedited (US): 1–2 business days (rate at checkout)
  • International: 7–14 business days; duties may apply

You'll receive tracking by email as soon as your order ships.

Returns

Every order is backed by our 90-day money-back guarantee. Not satisfied? Email hello@prairiefatco.example within 90 days for a full refund — keep the jar. Unopened multi-packs can be returned within 30 days.

Damaged orders

If anything arrives damaged, email us within 14 days with a photo and we'll make it right immediately.
