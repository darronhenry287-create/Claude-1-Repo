The whole list

Four ingredients. Traceable to the source.

No water, no fragrance, no fillers. Just four simple things your skin already knows how to use.

50% · The base

Grass-fed beef tallow

Rendered from the suet of grass-fed, pasture-raised cattle, tallow is remarkably close to the oils your own skin produces. That's why it absorbs cleanly and nourishes deeply instead of just sitting on top.

Rich in vitamins A, D, E, K and conjugated linoleic acid — the building blocks of a healthy skin barrier.

25% · The calm

Raw wildflower honey

A natural humectant, honey pulls moisture into the skin and holds it there. Its gentle antibacterial nature helps calm the look of redness and blemishes — sourced from small independent apiaries, never heat-treated.

15% · The shield

Golden beeswax

Beeswax forms a breathable barrier that seals hydration in and harsh weather out — without suffocating the skin. It's what gives the balm its satisfying, scoopable texture.

10% · The glow

Cold-pressed olive oil

Extra-virgin and cold-pressed to keep its antioxidants intact. Loaded with vitamin E and polyphenols, it softens deep dry patches and smooths skin texture over time.
