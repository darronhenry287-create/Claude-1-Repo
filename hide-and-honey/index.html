The Recipe

Small batch · makes one 60g jar

Ingredients

  • 30g grass-fed beef tallow
  • 15g raw wildflower honey
  • 9g golden beeswax
  • 6g cold-pressed olive oil

Method

  1. Gently melt the tallow & beeswax.
  2. Stir in honey & olive oil.
  3. Whip until cloud-soft & cooled.
  4. Hand-fill, cap, and ship to you.
