Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · A starting template, not legal advice. Tailor to your jurisdiction (GDPR/CCPA) before launch.

Hide & Honey Co. ("we") respects your privacy. This explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Order info: name, address, email, phone, and payment details (processed securely by our payment provider — we never store full card numbers).
  • Account info: login and order history if you create an account.
  • Usage data: pages visited, device/browser type, and cookies.

How we use it

  • To process and ship orders and provide support.
  • To send order updates and, if you opt in, marketing emails.
  • To improve our products and prevent fraud.

Sharing

Only with service providers who help us operate (payment, shipping, email, analytics). We do not sell your personal data.

Your rights

You may access, correct, delete or port your data and opt out of marketing. Email privacy@prairiefatco.example.
