Refund Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · A starting template — review with legal counsel before launch.

90-day guarantee

Single jars may be returned for a full refund within 90 days of delivery — even if used. Email hello@hideandhoney.example to begin; no need to ship the jar back.

Multi-packs & sets

Unopened additional jars in their original packaging may be returned within 30 days for a refund.

Processing

Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days of approval.

Non-refundable

  • Gift cards
  • Final-sale items (clearly marked)

Haven't seen your refund after 10 business days? Contact us and we'll trace it.
