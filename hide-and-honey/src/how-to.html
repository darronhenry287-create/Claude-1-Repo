How to use

Three seconds, head to toe.

01

Warm a little

Scoop a pea-sized amount and rub between clean fingertips until it melts to an oil.

02

Press it in

Smooth over slightly damp skin — face, lips, elbows, hands, scars, anywhere tight or dry.

03

Glow on

Morning, night, or whenever skin asks. A little truly goes a long way.

Pro tips

Applying after a shower or a splash of water helps the balm trap that moisture in — you'll need even less.
Start with a tiny amount. Tallow is concentrated; too much can feel greasy. Build up only if your skin wants it.
Gentle enough for kids' cheeks, hands and dry patches. Always patch-test new skin first.
