Our story

We stripped skincare back to honest food.

Hide & Honey started in a farmhouse kitchen, a slow cooker, and a stubborn question: why does skincare need thirty ingredients you can't pronounce?

Our founder grew up on a cattle farm where nothing went to waste. Grandma rendered tallow on the stove and rubbed it into chapped hands, cracked heels, and the family's wind-burned faces all winter long. It just worked.

Years later, staring at a bathroom shelf full of half-used creams — each with a label like a chemistry exam — that memory came back. So we went to the kitchen, sourced the best grass-fed tallow and raw honey we could find, and whipped them with beeswax and olive oil until the texture was perfect.

Four ingredients. Nothing else. We've never seen a reason to add a fifth.
