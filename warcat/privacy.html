Privacy policy.

Last updated: June 2026

This policy explains what we collect, why, and the control you have. We keep it short and we don't sell your data — yours or your cat's.

Information we collect

  • Order & account data — name, contact details, shipping address and order history.
  • Device & app data — if you use the WARCAT app: cleaning cycles, drawer status, and the per-cat weight and visit data the box records, so we can power your dashboard and alerts.
  • Site usage — basic analytics and cookies to understand and improve warcat.com.

How we use it

  • To fulfill orders, provide support and honor warranties and trials.
  • To run the app features you turn on, including health insights and notifications.
  • To improve our products and (only if you opt in) send you helpful updates.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information or your cat's health data. We share data only with the service providers needed to run our store and app (such as payment and shipping partners), under strict confidentiality.

Your choices

  • Access, correct or delete your data by contacting us.
  • Opt out of marketing at any time via the unsubscribe link.
  • Manage cookies through your browser settings.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@warcat.com.
