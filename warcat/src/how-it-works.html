How it works

Self-cleaning, explained.

No mystery, no gimmicks — just smart sensors, a slow-turning sift drum and a sealed drawer working together every time your cat visits.

1 · Your cat steps in

Precision weight sensors in the base register the moment a cat enters — and which cat it is, by weight. Nothing happens while they're inside; WARCAT simply notes the visit.

2 · A safe pause

When your cat leaves, a smart timer waits a few minutes in case they come back. Infrared and weight sensors then confirm the globe is empty before anything moves.

3 · A quiet sift & seal

The globe rotates slowly on a gear-damped motor — under 40 dB — sifting clumps through a screen and dropping them into a carbon-sealed drawer below. Clean litter falls back, leveled and ready.

4 · You stay informed

The app logs the cycle, updates each cat's health trends, and pings you only when the drawer's getting full. Empty the sealed liner about once a week — that's the entire job.

Inside the globe

Engineered to be gentle — on cats and ears.

The sift screen and slow rotation do the work that a scoop normally would, without crushing clumps or kicking up dust. The whole mechanism is built around quiet, careful motion.

  • Gear-damped motor keeps every cycle under 40 dB.
  • Fine sift screen separates clumps cleanly and levels the bed.
  • Carbon-sealed drawer traps odor the instant waste drops in.
Built-in safety

Weight, infrared and anti-pinch sensors must all agree the chamber is clear before the drum turns.

Instant reverse

If a cat hops in mid-cycle, WARCAT stops and reverses within a fraction of a second.
