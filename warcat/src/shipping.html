Shipping & returns

Fast, free, and easy to send back.

Free 2-day shipping on every WARCAT, and a 90-night trial behind it.

Shipping

  • Free 2-day shipping on all orders within the contiguous United States.
  • Orders placed before 2pm ET on a business day ship the same day.
  • You'll receive tracking by email the moment your order leaves our warehouse.
  • Alaska, Hawaii and international shipping are available at checkout where supported, with rates and times shown before you pay.

Returns

Changed your mind, or it's just not the right fit? You're covered by our 90-night home trial.

  • Contact our care team to start a return within 90 nights of delivery.
  • We'll email you a prepaid return label and arrange pickup for the box.
  • Once we receive it, your refund is issued within 5–10 business days.
  • Returns are free — we cover return shipping on the WARCAT box.

Damaged or wrong item

If anything arrives damaged or incorrect, let us know within 14 days and we'll send a replacement right away at no cost. See our refund policy for full details.
