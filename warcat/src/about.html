Our story

We got tired of scooping. So we engineered our way out.

WARCAT started with three cats, two engineers and one very full litter box on a Sunday night.

Why we built it

A chore that never ends deserved a real fix.

We loved our cats. We did not love the twice-daily scoop, the smell that crept into the apartment, or the nagging worry about whether everyone was eating, drinking and using the box normally.

The auto boxes we tried were loud, cramped, or felt cheap — and none of them actually told us anything useful about our cats. So we did what engineers do: we built the one we wished existed. Quiet enough to forget. Big enough for every cat. Smart enough to spot a problem before we could.
