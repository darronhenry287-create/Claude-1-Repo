Refund policy.

Last updated: June 2026

We want you and your cats to love WARCAT. If you're not satisfied, here's how refunds work.

90-night trial refunds

WARCAT smart litter boxes are eligible for a full refund within 90 nights of delivery under our home trial. Contact us to start a return; once the box is received, we'll refund the full purchase price to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

Accessories

Unopened accessories (mats, filters, liners, replacement parts) may be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Opened consumables are non-refundable for hygiene reasons unless defective.

Damaged or defective items

If your order arrives damaged or develops a defect covered by our warranty, we'll repair, replace or refund it at no cost to you.

How refunds are issued

  • Refunds go back to the original payment method.
  • Processing takes 5–10 business days after we receive your return; your bank may take a little longer to post it.
  • Original shipping was free, and we cover return shipping on the box.

Questions

Email care@warcat.com and a real person will help you sort it out.
