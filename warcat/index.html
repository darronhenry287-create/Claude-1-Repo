How it works

Four steps. Zero effort.

Sensors, a slow-turning sift drum and a sealed drawer do the whole job — quietly, and only when it's safe.

Your cat visits

Precision weight sensors know the moment a cat steps in — and exactly when they leave.

A safe pause

WARCAT waits a few minutes, double-checks the chamber is empty, then begins.

Quiet sift & seal

The drum rotates slowly, sifting clumps into a carbon-sealed drawer below — under 40 dB.

You get a ping

The app logs the cycle and tells you when the drawer's full. Empty it weekly. Done.

Low-noise drive

A gear-damped motor runs the cycle under 40 dB — quieter than a fridge hum. It won't scare a shy cat or wake the house at 3am.
