How to take creatine — the easy way

Creatine is one of the most studied supplements there is. Taking it shouldn't be the hard part. Here's everything you need to know — it takes about thirty seconds a day.

1

Take 4 a day

Four gummies give you the full 5g dose of creatine monohydrate. Any time of day, with or without food.

2

Stay consistent

Creatine works by saturating your muscles, so the whole secret is simply taking it every day.

3

Feel the difference

Within two to four weeks, most people notice stronger lifts, faster recovery and steadier energy.

How much should I take?

5g of creatine monohydrate per day — that's four FORM gummies. It's the dose used in the majority of creatine research, and it's the amount your muscles can actually put to work. No half-scoops, no guesswork.

When's the best time to take it?

Honestly, whenever you'll remember. Morning with coffee, after a workout, with dinner — the timing barely matters. What matters is taking your four gummies consistently, day after day. Pick a moment that's already part of your routine and anchor it there.

Do I need to "load" creatine?

No loading phase is required. Just take your four gummies daily and your muscles will reach full saturation over about three to four weeks. If you'd like to feel the effects a little sooner, you can optionally double up to eight gummies a day for the first week, then settle back to four.

How long until I notice results?

Most people feel a difference in two to four weeks, as creatine builds up in the muscle. Strength, power and recovery tend to show up first; many also report steadier daily energy. Stick with it — consistency is what unlocks the benefits.

Can I take it on rest days?

Yes — take FORM every day, training day or not. Because creatine works by keeping your muscles saturated over time, daily intake matters more than whether you worked out. Rest days count too.
