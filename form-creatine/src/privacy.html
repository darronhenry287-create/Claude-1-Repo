Last updated: June 2026

Overview

CREO Nutrition ("CREO", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our store or place an order, how we use it, and the choices you have. This page is provided for general information and isn't legal advice.

Information we collect

  • Contact details — your name, email address, phone number, and shipping address.
  • Order information — what you bought, order history, and subscription preferences.
  • Payment information — processed securely through Shopify and our payment providers. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Browsing data — pages you view, device and browser information, and cookies, as described below.

How we use your information

  • To process and fulfill your orders and subscriptions.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your messages.
  • To send marketing emails when you've given consent — you can opt out anytime.
  • To understand and improve how our store performs.

Sharing your information

We share information only with trusted service providers who help us run the business — such as Shopify (our store platform), payment processors, and shipping carriers — and only as needed to serve you. We may also disclose information if required by law. We never sell your personal information.

Cookies & analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep your cart working, remember your preferences, and measure how the store is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not work without them.

Your rights

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or unsubscribe from marketing at any time. Just email hello@trycreo.com and we'll help.

Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is ever completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

Children

Our store and products are not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
