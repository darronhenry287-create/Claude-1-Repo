How it started

It began with a cupboard full of half-used tubs.

Between us, we'd bought creatine more times than we'd like to admit. Every time it was the same story: a big plastic tub, a chalky scoop, a shaker left to fester in the sink. We'd use it diligently for a couple of weeks, then watch it slowly migrate to the back of the cupboard behind the protein no one finished.

The research was never in doubt. Creatine monohydrate works — it's about as close to a sure thing as supplements get. The dose was clear, too: five grams a day, every day. The only thing standing between us and the benefits was the part nobody talks about. The mixing. The grit. The forgetting.

So we made the version we'd actually keep on the counter.

The full clinical 5g dose, in a gummy worth reaching for. No powder, no shaker, no clumps at the bottom of the tub. Just four gummies a day that taste like something you'd want anyway — so the habit finally sticks. That's the whole idea behind FORM.
