Our story

Creatine you'll actually keep taking.

We didn't set out to reinvent creatine. We set out to fix the only part that was broken — actually remembering to take it.

How it started

It began with a cupboard full of half-used tubs.

The research on creatine is overwhelming and boring in the best way: it works. For strength, for power, for recovery — and increasingly for focus and healthy aging. The science was never the problem.

The problem was the tub. The chalky scoop. The shaker in the sink. Every one of us had bought creatine, used it for a few weeks, and let it migrate to the back of a cupboard. So we made the version we'd actually keep on the counter: the full 5g dose, in a gummy worth reaching for.
