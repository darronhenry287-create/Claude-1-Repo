From the founder

I wanted a firming oil I'd actually keep using.

I'm Camille. For years I rotated through firming creams, tightening serums and stretch-mark oils — a whole shelf of them. Some were tacky. Most were drenched in perfume. None of them stuck, because using them was a chore.

It turned out the science wasn't the hard part. Caffeine-rich botanicals have firmed the look of skin for decades. The hard part was the feel — building something dry-touch and fragrance-free that you'd genuinely want to reach for every single day.

So we obsessed over the base until the oil disappeared into skin in seconds. Because the best firming routine is the one you don't have to talk yourself into.

How we got here

A slow, deliberate road.

2021

The greasy-towel problem

Frustrated by oils that never sank in, I started sketching what a fast-absorbing, fragrance-free firming oil would actually look like.

2022

31 formulas later

We worked with a cosmetic chemist through 31 versions, tuning the squalane-and-jojoba base until the dry-touch finish was right and the actives were dialled in.

2023

Dermatologist-tested, then shipped

Once it passed testing — non-comedogenic, gentle enough for daily and post-partum use — we sent the first bottles to a small group of testers. They reordered within weeks.

Today

6,400+ firmer mornings

One bottle, one minute. Same clean formula, now part of thousands of daily rituals — and a 4.9-star average we don't take for granted.

Our promise

Four things we'll never do.

  • We'll never hide a synthetic perfume behind the word "fragrance."
  • We'll never lean on silicones for a firmness you can only feel for an hour.
  • We'll never claim to cure anything — only to visibly firm, smooth and nourish.
  • We'll never make a single bottle we wouldn't use ourselves, daily.
