Our story

It started with grandma's tin.

No lab. No focus groups. Just a farmhouse kitchen, a slow cooker, and a stubborn belief that skincare got needlessly complicated.

How we got here

A slow, honest road.

'94

The tin by the sink

Grandma kept rendered tallow by the kitchen sink — for chapped hands, wind-burned cheeks, scraped knees. It fixed everything.

2021

47 messy batches

Staring at a shelf of $60 creams we couldn't pronounce, we went back to her recipe — and whipped it 47 times until the texture was perfect.

2022

The first 100 jars

We hand-filled a hundred jars at the kitchen table and gave them to friends. They came back asking for more — and bringing theirs.

Today

8,200 happy faces

Same four ingredients. Same small batches. Just a lot more sinks with a jar beside them.

Our promise

Four things we'll never do.

  • We'll never add water to cut costs.
  • We'll never hide behind the word "fragrance."
  • We'll never put a fifth ingredient in just to sound fancy.
  • We'll never make a claim our own skin can't back up.

"We didn't set out to start a skincare company. We just couldn't go back to complicated once we'd tried simple."

— Jess, founder
