Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · A starting template, not legal advice. Tailor to your jurisdiction (GDPR/CCPA) before launch.

LISSE ("LISSE", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you visit our store or buy the LISSE Firming Body Oil, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our site you agree to the practices described here.

What we collect

  • Order information: your name, shipping and billing address, email, phone number, and payment details. Payments are processed securely by our payment and checkout providers — we never see or store full card numbers.
  • Account & subscription information: if you create an account or start a Subscribe & Save plan, we keep your order history and delivery schedule so you can manage, skip or cancel it.
  • Support information: the messages, order numbers and any photos you send us when you contact hello@lisse.com.
  • Usage data: pages visited, products viewed, device and browser type, and cookies or similar technologies that help the site work and let us understand what's useful.

How we use it

  • To process, ship and track your order, and to manage subscriptions and our 90-day promise.
  • To provide customer care and respond to your questions.
  • To send order and shipping updates, and — only if you opt in — skin notes and marketing emails. You can unsubscribe from marketing at any time.
  • To improve our products and storefront, prevent fraud, and meet our legal obligations.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to run the cart and checkout, and analytics cookies to understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some may affect how the store works.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers who help us operate — payment processing, checkout, shipping carriers, email, reviews and analytics — and only as needed to do their job. We may also disclose information where required by law. We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We retain order and account information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service, honour the 90-day promise, and meet tax and legal requirements. After that, it's deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to opt out of marketing or the "sale" of personal information. To make a request, email hello@lisse.com and we'll respond within the time required by law.

Children

LISSE is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes & contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above will always reflect the current version. Questions about your privacy? Email hello@lisse.com.
