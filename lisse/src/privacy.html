Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

LISSE ("LISSE", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you visit our store or buy the LISSE Firming Body Oil, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using our site you agree to these practices.

Information we collect

  • Order information: your name, shipping and billing address, email, phone, and what you buy. Payments are processed securely through Shopify and our payment providers — we never store full card numbers.
  • Account & subscription information: if you create an account or start a Subscribe & Save plan, your order history and delivery schedule, so you can manage, skip or cancel it.
  • Support information: the messages, order numbers and photos you send when you contact hello@lisse.com.
  • Usage & device data: pages viewed, device and browser type, and cookies or similar technologies, as described below.

How we use your information

  • To process, ship and track orders, and manage subscriptions and our 90-day promise.
  • To provide customer care and handle returns or refunds.
  • To send order and shipping updates, and — only if you opt in — marketing emails, with a one-click unsubscribe in every one.
  • To improve our store, prevent fraud, and meet our legal and tax obligations.

Cookies & analytics

We use essential cookies to keep your cart and checkout working and to remember preferences, and analytics cookies to measure how the store is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not work without them.

How we share information

We share information only with the service providers who help us run the business — Shopify, payment processors, shipping carriers, and email and analytics tools — and only as much as they need. We may also disclose information where required by law. We never sell your personal information.

Data retention & security

We keep order and account information for as long as your account is active or as needed to honour the 90-day promise and meet tax and legal requirements; after that it's deleted or anonymised. We protect it with reasonable measures, including encryption in transit and PCI-compliant payment processing. No method is ever completely secure, but we work hard to keep your data safe.

Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to opt out of marketing or the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (including under the GDPR and CCPA). Email hello@lisse.com to make a request — we'll respond within the time the law requires and won't discriminate against you for asking.

Children & international transfers

LISSE is intended for adults; we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. We're based in the United States, and providers acting on our behalf may process data in the US or other countries, always in line with this policy.

Changes & contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above reflects the current version. Questions, or want to make a request? Visit our contact page or email hello@lisse.com and we'll reply within one business day.
