Shipping & Returns

Last updated: June 2026

Here's everything you need to know about how and when your LISSE Firming Body Oil reaches you — and what happens if something isn't right. Have a question we haven't covered? Get in touch and a real person will help.

Where we ship

We ship throughout the United States from our US fulfilment centre, and we also offer international shipping to select countries. If checkout lets you add your address, we can deliver to you.

Processing & delivery times

Orders are dispatched within 1 business day. Orders placed over a weekend or on a holiday go out the next business day. Once dispatched, US orders typically arrive within 2–5 business days, depending on your location. You'll receive an order confirmation right away.

Shipping costs

Standard shipping is free on orders over $50. For orders under $50, a flat rate of $4.95 applies. Shipping is calculated and shown at checkout before you pay, so there are no surprises.

Tracking

As soon as your order ships, we'll email you a tracking link so you can follow your bottle all the way to your door. If you don't see it, check your spam folder or reach out and we'll resend it.

Subscriptions

Subscribe & Save orders ship on your chosen schedule at the same free-over-$50 rates. You can skip, reschedule or cancel anytime from your account before your next dispatch.

International orders

International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on your destination. Please note that duties, taxes and customs fees may apply on arrival and are the responsibility of the recipient. Delivery times vary by country and customs processing.

Returns

Every order is backed by our 90-day money-back promise. If you don't love it, email hello@lisse.com within 90 days of delivery for a full refund — no need to ship the bottle back, and you keep it. Unopened multi-packs can be returned within 30 days. For the full details, see our Refund Policy. And if anything arrives damaged or your tracking stalls, email us with your order number and a photo — we'll make it right straight away.

Questions

Still need a hand with an order, a delivery or a return? Visit our contact page or email hello@lisse.com and we'll get back to you within one business day.
