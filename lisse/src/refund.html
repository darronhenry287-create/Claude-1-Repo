Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2026

We want you to love how your skin feels with LISSE Firming Body Oil. If it isn't right for you, getting your money back should be simple — no hoops, no hassle, and no need to ship anything back. Here's exactly how it works.

Our 90-day promise

Your first bottle of LISSE Firming Body Oil is covered by our 90-day money-back promise. If you don't love it, request a full refund within 90 days of delivery — even if the bottle is used. Email hello@lisse.com to begin; there's no need to ship the bottle back, and you keep it.

How to start a refund

Email hello@lisse.com with your order number (it's in your confirmation email) and a quick note that you'd like a refund, within 90 days of delivery. That's it — we'll take care of the rest and confirm by email.

Multi-packs & the 3-Bottle Ritual

Your first opened bottle is fully covered by the 90-day promise above. Any additional unopened bottles in their original packaging — including those in a 3-Bottle Ritual — may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund of those units.

Subscriptions

You're in control of your Subscribe & Save plan: skip, reschedule or cancel anytime from your account before your next dispatch. Orders already shipped follow the same refund terms above. We don't charge cancellation or restocking fees.

How refunds are processed

Once your refund is approved — usually within one business day — it's issued to your original payment method. Banks and card providers typically post the funds within 5–10 business days. We'll never charge a restocking or processing fee.

Damaged, wrong or lost items

If your order arrives damaged, you received the wrong item, or your tracking stalls, we're sorry — that's on us. Email hello@lisse.com with your order number and a photo if you can, and we'll send a free replacement or full refund right away. As a single-product brand we don't run formal exchanges, but if something isn't right, reach out and we'll sort it.

Non-refundable

  • Gift cards
  • Final-sale items (clearly marked at purchase)

Questions

Haven't seen your refund after 10 business days, or need a hand? Visit our contact page or email hello@lisse.com with your order number and we'll trace it for you within one business day.
