Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · A starting template — review with legal counsel before launch.

Our 90-day promise

Your first bottle of LISSE Firming Body Oil is covered by our 90-day money-back promise. If you don't love it, you can request a full refund within 90 days of delivery — even if the bottle is used. Email hello@lisse.com to begin; there's no need to ship the bottle back, and you keep it.

Multi-packs & the 3-Bottle Ritual

Your first opened bottle is fully covered by the promise above. Any additional unopened bottles in their original packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund of those units.

Subscriptions

You can skip, reschedule or cancel a Subscribe & Save plan anytime from your account. Orders already shipped follow the same refund terms above. We don't charge cancellation or restocking fees.

How refunds are processed

Once your refund is approved — usually within one business day — it's issued to your original payment method. Banks typically post the funds within 5–10 business days.

Non-refundable

  • Gift cards
  • Final-sale items (clearly marked at purchase)

Haven't seen your refund after 10 business days? Contact us with your order number and we'll trace it for you.
