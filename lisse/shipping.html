Shipping & Returns

Last updated: June 2026 · A starting template — review before launch.

Processing & dispatch

We pack and dispatch orders within 1–2 business days. You'll receive a confirmation when you order and a tracking link by email the moment your LISSE is on its way.

Shipping rates

Enjoy free shipping on orders over $50 within the continental US. Orders under $50 ship at a flat $4.95. Every 3-Bottle Ritual ships free, always.

  • Standard (US): 3–5 business days
  • Expedited (US): 1–2 business days (rate shown at checkout)
  • International: 7–14 business days; any customs duties or import taxes are the recipient's responsibility

Subscriptions

Subscribe & Save orders ship on your chosen schedule (every 1–2 months) at the same free-over-$50 rates. You can skip, reschedule or cancel anytime from your account before your next dispatch.

Returns

Every order is backed by our 90-day money-back promise. If you don't love it, email hello@lisse.com within 90 days of delivery for a full refund — no need to ship the bottle back, and you keep it. Unopened multi-packs can be returned within 30 days. See our Refund Policy for details.

Damaged or missing orders

If anything arrives damaged, or your tracking stalls, email us within 14 days with your order number and a photo. We'll make it right straight away — no fuss.
