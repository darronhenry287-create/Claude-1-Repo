Good to know

Questions, answered.

Sleep on it for 100 nights. If it's not the best pillow you've owned, we'll arrange a free return and full refund — no shipping fees, no awkward questions.
The Cloud Pillow is dual-height — just flip it. The taller side suits side sleepers and broader shoulders; the lower side suits back and stomach sleepers. Most people switch within the first week to find their fit.
The CloudCool™ gel layer is phase-change material — it absorbs and disperses body heat to hold the surface ~4°C cooler than standard memory foam. The Tencel cover adds breathability on top.
Unzip the Tencel cover and machine wash cold, tumble dry low. The foam core should not be submerged — spot clean only.
