Our story

We were tired of being tired.

SOMNA began with a simple, stubborn belief: that the single thing you spend a third of your life on shouldn't be an afterthought.

In 2021, our founders — a sleep researcher and a product designer — kept having the same conversation. Why did every pillow on the market fall flat within months? Why did "cooling" pillows trap heat by 2am? Why did you have to choose between support and comfort?

So they did what obsessives do. They built one. Then another. Forty-seven prototypes later, tested in a sleep lab and on a lot of very tired volunteers, The Cloud Pillow was born — and SOMNA with it.
