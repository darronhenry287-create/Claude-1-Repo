// The standard

From raw material to your door.

We source pharmaceutical-grade β-NMN and verify identity and purity before anything is encapsulated. Manufacturing happens in a cGMP-certified facility. Then every finished lot goes back out to an ISO 17025-accredited lab — a separate set of eyes — to confirm what's on the label is what's in the bottle.

Heavy metals, microbials, residual solvents, assay purity: all checked, all documented. The QR code on your bottle pulls up that exact lot's certificate. No "proprietary blend" hand-waving, no trust-me-bro.

Why it matters

  • NMN is fragile and easy to adulterate — testing is the only real safeguard.
  • Underdosing is the industry's quiet default; the full dose is the whole point.
  • Transparency should be the floor, not a premium feature.

That's the entire company, really: do the boring, expensive things right, and show our work.
