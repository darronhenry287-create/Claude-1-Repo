The coenzyme

What is NAD⁺?

Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD⁺) is a coenzyme present in every living cell. It's the shuttle that carries electrons through metabolism, and the fuel that key repair and signalling enzymes literally consume to do their jobs.

Without enough NAD⁺, mitochondria make less energy, sirtuins go quiet, and DNA-repair enzymes run short on what they need. It is, quite literally, a molecule of cellular currency.

The decline

Why levels fall.

Production slows and consumption climbs. By midlife, NAD⁺ can sit at roughly half its youthful level — a shift researchers link to many hallmarks of aging.
