Refund policy.

We want you to feel great about your purchase — literally. This policy explains how refunds work alongside our 60-night guarantee.

60-night money-back guarantee

Try AEON daily for up to 60 nights from delivery. If you're not satisfied, email us for a full refund of your first order of that product. You don't need to return what's left.

Standard returns

Unopened, unused products may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund to your original payment method, less original shipping. Email care@aeonlabs.example to begin.

Subscriptions

You can skip, pause or cancel anytime before an order ships from your account. Charges for orders that have already shipped fall under the guarantee above rather than automatic reversal.

How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are processed within 3–5 business days. Depending on your bank, it may take a few additional days to appear on your statement.
