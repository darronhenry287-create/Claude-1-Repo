Privacy policy.

Your trust is the product. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the control you have.

What we collect

  • Order details: name, shipping/billing address, email, and items purchased.
  • Payment data, processed securely by our payment provider — we never store full card numbers.
  • Usage data: basic analytics about how the site is used, to improve it.

How we use it

To fulfill and support your orders, manage subscriptions, prevent fraud, and — only if you opt in — send you product and protocol emails. You can unsubscribe anytime.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information. We share data only with the processors needed to run the store (payments, shipping, email), under contract.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at privacy@aeonlabs.example. We honor applicable privacy rights including GDPR and CCPA.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to run the cart and checkout, plus optional analytics cookies you can decline.

This is a demo storefront; no real orders are processed and no personal data is collected.
