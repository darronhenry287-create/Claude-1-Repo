Shipping & returns.

Shipping

Orders placed before 2pm ET on a business day ship the same day; otherwise the next business day. You'll get tracking by email as soon as your order leaves our facility.

  • Free carbon-neutral shipping on orders over $50.
  • Flat $4.95 shipping under $50.
  • Subscriptions always ship free, via priority.
  • US delivery typically 2–5 business days; international 5–14 business days.

International

We ship to most countries. Any import duties or taxes are calculated at checkout where possible, or collected by your local carrier on delivery.

Returns

Unopened products can be returned within 30 days for a refund. Opened products are covered by our 60-night money-back guarantee — no return shipping required. To start either, email care@aeonlabs.example with your order number.

Lost or damaged

If your order arrives damaged or doesn't arrive at all, contact us within 14 days and we'll replace it or refund you, no fuss.
